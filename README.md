# PeerConnect

**PeerConnect** is a full-stack **campus networking and collaboration platform** built to help students easily discover, connect, communicate, and collaborate with other students within their college community.

The main idea is to provide a **college-focused networking space** where students can create professional profiles, find peers with similar skills and interests, build connections, communicate in real time, share updates, and discover useful opportunities such as projects, hackathons, mentorships, and collaborations.

---

## What PeerConnect Offers

- **Student Profiles** – Create detailed profiles with department, academic year, bio, skills, interests, experience level, social links, and profile picture.
- **Student Discovery** – Browse and search for other students based on their profiles and interests.
- **Peer Recommendations** – Get relevant peer suggestions using factors such as skill compatibility, department, academic year, and activity.
- **Connections** – Send, accept, reject, withdraw, and remove connections to build a personal campus network.
- **Real-Time Chat** – Connected students can communicate through one-to-one messaging using Socket.io, with features such as typing indicators and online status.
- **Community Feed** – Students can create, edit, and delete posts and interact through likes and comments.
- **Opportunities Board** – Provides a space for discovering campus-focused projects, hackathons, mentorships, and collaboration opportunities.
- **Notifications** – Users receive notifications for connection requests, accepted connections, likes, and comments, with unread notification tracking.
- **Interactive Dashboard** – Displays network statistics, skill matches, activity, achievements, recommendations, community content, and opportunities.
- **Responsive UI** – Designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices with both light and dark themes.

---

## Technology Used

**Frontend:** React.js, React Router, Tailwind CSS, Axios, Chart.js, Lucide React

**Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, Socket.io, JWT, bcrypt.js

**Database:** MongoDB with Mongoose

**Deployment:** Vercel, Render, MongoDB Atlas

## Objective

The objective of PeerConnect is to create a centralized digital networking ecosystem for students, making it easier to find suitable peers, build meaningful connections, communicate, collaborate on projects, and discover opportunities within their college community.

---

## Future Scope

The platform can be further extended with AI-powered peer and opportunity matching, group chats, project workspaces, college clubs, events, institutional verification, admin dashboards, and advanced analytics.
